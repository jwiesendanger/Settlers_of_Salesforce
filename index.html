    Pick a tile

    Tap a teammate's hex to see their tasks.

    Each hex is a teammate. Its terrain shows which resource dominates their queue: hills for Brick, mountains for Ore, pasture for Wool, desert for an empty queue. The number token counts their Brick plus Wool (active work, leaving out aging Ore), the pips under it count Brick, the stack of gold coins counts work they finished in the last 5 business days, and the robber sits on whoever holds the most Ore. Click any block to comment on that task in Asana.

    How the Guild Hall works
    1. Grant a responsibility Choose a hero in the bar below, type the responsibility and press Grant. You can also click any hero to open their character sheet.
    2. Revoke a responsibility Click the hero, then press Revoke next to the responsibility and confirm.
    3. Recruit a hero Press Recruit a hero, then enter their name, title, class and who they report to.
    4. Dismiss a hero Click the hero and use Dismiss from the guild at the bottom of their sheet. Dismissed heroes wait in the tavern and can be brought back.

    Connect to Asana

    This page reads the team's tasks straight from Asana with your own Asana access, so you only see what you can already see in Asana, and comments post under your name.

    1. Open Asana's developer console (sign in to Asana if asked).
    2. Choose Create new token, name it Settlers, and agree to the terms.
    3. Copy the token and paste it here.

    Your token stays in this browser and is only sent to Asana. On a shared computer, press Disconnect at the top of the page when you're done. You can revoke the token anytime in Asana's developer console.
